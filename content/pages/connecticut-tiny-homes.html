Connecticut Tiny Homes, accessory dwelling units for backyard living

ADU specialist Full-service: zoning to construction Connecticut & surrounding areas

If a full accessory dwelling unit is more what you have in mind than a home on wheels, Connecticut Tiny Homes specializes exactly there.

Connecticut Tiny Homes, also known as Contemporary Tiny Homes, focuses on accessory dwelling units, permanent structures built onto an existing property, serving Connecticut and surrounding areas. They manage the full process from zoning to design to construction. Their ADUs typically range from $100,000 to $250,000 depending on size and design.

Who it's for

Homeowners looking to add a permanent, high value structure to their property, like a rental unit or space for family, rather than a portable tiny home.
