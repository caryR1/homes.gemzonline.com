Builder spotlight

Craftsman Tiny Homes, handcrafted tiny living from Florida

Family owned since 1976 Ships nationwide Archer, Florida

For buyers who want a tiny home that feels like it was truly built by hand, not stamped out of a factory, Craftsman Tiny Homes is worth a serious look.

Craftsman Tiny Homes is a family owned builder based in Archer, Florida, near Gainesville, with construction roots going back to 1976. The family shifted from home remodeling into building tiny houses, carrying that traditional, hands-on approach into every build. Their lineup includes models like the Nordic, a 24 foot home with lofted ceilings and pine siding for a European inspired look. Homes ship nationwide.

Who it's for

Buyers who value traditional craftsmanship, a smaller family run builder, and a warm, cabin-like aesthetic over a big factory operation.
