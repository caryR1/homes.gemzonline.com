Frequently asked questions

Questions we hear a lot from tiny home buyers

Do I need a real estate license to sell my current home before buying a tiny home?

No, selling your own primary residence doesn't require a license. That requirement only comes up for people acting as an agent or broker for someone else's transaction, which is why we screen out affiliate programs on this site that would require us, or you, to hold one.

Is a tiny home on wheels legally allowed as my only home?

It depends entirely on your local zoning code, not on the builder. Some areas allow it outright, some only allow it as a temporary or seasonal structure, and some don't allow it at all as a primary residence. Check with your local planning department before you buy. Our zoning laws post walks through exactly what to ask.

What's the real difference between a tiny home and an ADU?

A tiny home on wheels is mobile and generally cheaper, an ADU is a permanent structure on a foundation that adds appraised value to your property but costs significantly more. We break the full comparison down, including where modular family homes fit in, in Tiny Home vs ADU vs Modular Family Home.

Can I finance a tiny home with a normal mortgage?

Usually not, if it's built on wheels. Most buyers use personal loans or RV loans for mobile tiny homes, and construction loans or home equity financing for permanent ADUs. Details are in our financing guide.

Do the builders on this site pay to be listed?

No. Builders don't pay for placement. We may earn an affiliate commission if you buy after clicking through to a builder, which is disclosed on every spotlight page and in our About page, but that's separate from, and doesn't influence, which builders we choose to cover.

Why don't some spotlight pages show exact pricing?

Several of the builders we cover only quote pricing directly to buyers, based on the specific model, finishes, and site conditions. Rather than publish a guessed number, we tell you it's quote-only and point you to the builder to get a real figure.

Can I earn money by referring people to this site?

Yes. Anyone can become an affiliate for free, no real estate license or experience required. You pick a builder to promote, get a personal tracking link the moment you sign up, and earn a cut when someone buys through it. Your dashboard shows your clicks, sales, and earnings.
