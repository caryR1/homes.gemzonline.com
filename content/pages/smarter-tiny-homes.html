Smarter Tiny Homes, custom-built tiny living from San Diego

Custom quote No license required Solar-ready

For buyers who want old-world craftsmanship paired with modern, off-grid-ready features, Smarter Tiny Homes builds custom tiny homes out of the San Diego area.

Smarter Tiny Homes is a family owned builder with decades of experience, offering four named models: The Palisade, The Newport, The Malibu, and The Bel Air, ranging from compact designs optimized for mobility to larger models built for full-time living. Homes are built with premium materials and joinery methods, including durable metal roofing and premium luxury vinyl plank flooring, using eco-friendly, locally sourced materials where possible. Every home is solar-ready, with pre-installed conduit and hookups for off-grid power, and comes with a one-year warranty on labor and materials. Finishes, fixtures, and appliances can be fully customized to the buyer's preferences.

Who it's for

Buyers on the West Coast who want a fully custom, solar-ready tiny home built with premium materials, and who are comfortable going through a direct quote process rather than picking from published pricing.
