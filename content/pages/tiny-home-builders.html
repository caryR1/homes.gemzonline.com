Tiny Home Builders, real house plans for buyers who want to build it themselves

Plans, not finished builds 96–260 sq ft 1,800+ plans sold 14-day guarantee

Not every buyer wants a finished home delivered to their lot, some want to build it themselves, on their own timeline, with their own hands. Tiny Home Builders is built for exactly that buyer: they sell detailed, buildable plans, not completed houses.

Tiny Home Builders offers five Tiny House on Wheels plan sets, Tiny Living, The Element, Tiny Studio, Tiny Retirement, and Simple Living, ranging from about 96 to 260 square feet, plus two backyard studio shed designs, Backyard Studio and Tiny Playhouse. Each plan set includes large format 18 by 12 inch printed construction sheets, a PDF download, an editable SketchUp 3D model you or your architect can customize, electrical diagrams showing outlet and switch placement, and a comprehensive itemized material list. They've sold over 1,800 sets of plans and back every purchase with a 14 day money back guarantee. For buyers who want guidance beyond the paper, they also run hands-on and online workshops teaching the actual build techniques professionals use.

Who it's for

Hands-on buyers who want to build, or hire a local contractor to build, their own tiny home rather than purchase a finished unit, a fundamentally different, lower cost entry point than any full build on this site.
